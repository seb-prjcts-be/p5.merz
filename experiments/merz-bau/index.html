Säule

A column that grows. Each year adds a few letters, letter parts and one found piece. Every third year, white plaster covers what is there. The earlier years stay inside as grottoes; some openings remain. Drag to turn the column, move the year to watch it grow, move the cut to take the plaster away.

This is a 3D study in WEBGL mode, built with the current library: the letter paths from satz() and bauteil() become beams, a fundsachen() image becomes a textured plane. The plaster and the growth rule are local to this sketch.
